About Me _ ▢ ×

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at CU Boulder, currently working through coursework like MCEN 3030 (Computational Methods) alongside hands-on project work — most recently a Ryobi-drill-powered vehicle for my Component Design course, where I serve as the systems and testing engineer.

I like projects that go all the way from a CAD model to a physical object you can actually test: modeling a full Nintendo Switch 2 assembly in SolidWorks to sharpen my drafting skills, or building an Arduino-based people-counting system with a small team to solve a real problem on campus.

Outside of engineering coursework, I write and edit video — you can find both in the Writing and Video sections of this site.
